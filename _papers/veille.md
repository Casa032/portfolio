title: Machine learning or deep learning
---

This paper focuses on the different families of artificial intelligence, primarily deep learning and its role in the success of other AI branches. This work also emphasizes the necessity of understanding AI as a whole, in all its complexity, rather than reducing it to a particular domain. This is the only approach if we want to develop more robust systems that are better adapted to real-world problems.
